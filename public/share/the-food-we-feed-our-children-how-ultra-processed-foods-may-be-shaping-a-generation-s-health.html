The Food We Feed Our Children: How Ultra-Processed Foods May Be Shaping a Generation’s Health

From breakfast cereals and packaged snacks to sugary drinks, instant noodles and ready-to-eat meals, ultra-processed foods are becoming an increasingly familiar part of childhood. The concern is not simply that children are eating more junk food. It is that these foods may be changing the way children eat, setting the stage for obesity and metabolic disease much earlier in life.
